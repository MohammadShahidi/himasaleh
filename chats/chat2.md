- **Model:** we're a marketplace. The customer pays us online into an escrow account. After the waybill is signed and the customer confirms delivery, we deduct our commission and pay out the supplier's share and the driver's net fare.

- **Cash on delivery (پس‌کرایه):** the driver's commission is deducted from their wallet. A driver needs a minimum wallet balance to accept a load.
- **Supplying from our own stock later:** a switch for "our own stock", where we earn a margin instead of a commission.

I'm building the admin finance panel, the supplier panel, the supplier role in login, and the commission breakdown in the driver panel.
